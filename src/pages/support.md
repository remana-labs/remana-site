# Support

<div class="prose">

Write to [hello@remana.ai](mailto:hello@remana.ai). During testing a person reads every message,
usually within a day.

## What leaves my phone?

The audio you record, for speech-to-text and speaker recognition, and the transcript, for extraction.
Your memory, transcripts and voiceprints stay on the phone. The full picture is in the
[privacy policy](/privacy).

## How do I delete a meeting?

Delete it in the app. It is removed from your phone, and there is no copy on our servers to remove.

## Does signing out delete anything?

No. Signing out removes the access token. Everything on the phone stays, and capture, search and
on-device briefings keep working. Turning recordings into notes needs an account again.
