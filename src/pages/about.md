# About

<div class="prose">

Meetings produce two kinds of knowledge: what people actually said, and what you conclude from it.
Every tool we tried blends them into one confident summary, so you can never tell which is which —
and the moment you act on an inference as if it were a fact, the tool has cost you more than it
saved.

Remana keeps the two apart on purpose. It records on your phone, turns the recording into memory —
facts, commitments, open questions, per person — and marks each item as said or inferred. It
remembers across meetings, so the next time you sit down with someone, what you agreed last time is
already in front of you. The memory lives on your phone; the cloud only does the listening.

We are building the memory first and the business second: a memory that a stranger can trust has to
exist before anything is charged for it.

Remana is built by Reza and a small group of collaborators. Say hello at
[hello@remana.ai](mailto:hello@remana.ai).
