# Press kit

<div class="prose">

Remana is a meeting memory that knows what was said, and what it only inferred. Contact:
[hello@remana.ai](mailto:hello@remana.ai).

## The mark

An opening quotation mark. The solid comma is what was said; the hollow comma is what was inferred.

## Colours

| use | value |
|---|---|
| icon ground | `#192F2F` |
| solid comma | `#2DD4BF` |
| hollow comma | `#9BA3A8` |

## Three rules

1. Do not recolour the solid comma; it is the one accent.
2. The wordmark is `remana`, lowercase, never set in the accent colour.
3. Keep clear space of one comma-head around the mark.
